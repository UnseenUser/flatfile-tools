Flat File

A few things that live on the open web rather than behind a download.

Cut list calculator
Cutting layouts for sheet goods, with the saw kerf subtracted and the grain respected. Runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Free, up to ten parts.
Linear cut optimizer
Cutting plans for bar, pipe, tube, angle and rebar, with the saw kerf taken off every cut and the offcuts worth keeping marked. Runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
Sheetgrab privacy policy
What the Chrome extension collects, which is nothing, and the one host it is allowed to contact.
The paid versions are at flatfile.gumroad.com.
